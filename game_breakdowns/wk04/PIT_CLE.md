## 5. THREAT INTELLIGENCE

Football Intel's deterministic Threat Engine flags a player when two things converge in the same statistical category: the player's own **season rank** clears a tier's threshold, and the **upcoming opponent's defensive rank in that same category** is weak enough to clear that tier's own threshold on the other side. Neither signal alone is enough — it's the intersection that matters. The three tiers use these exact, fixed thresholds (player rank is 1st-best; defense rank is 1st-toughest, so a high defense-rank number means a weak defense):

- **Nuclear** — player ranks top 3 in the category AND the opponent's defense ranks 30th or worse in that same category
- **Elite** — player ranks top 5 AND opponent defense ranks 28th or worse
- **Standard** — player ranks top 10 AND opponent defense ranks 23rd or worse

"Double," "Triple," and "Quadruple" describe how many categories contributed: a Double is one category meeting both thresholds; a Triple adds a category where the player's own rank clears the bar even if the defense side doesn't fully qualify; a Quadruple means two or more categories fully converge on both sides.
