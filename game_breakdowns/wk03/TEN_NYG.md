**Finding 2: Both defenses have swapped which unit is their real strength since 2025, in mirrored directions — and neither team's offensive approach appears to have adjusted to the other's new weakness yet.** New York's run defense has improved dramatically (31st in 2025 to 20th through two 2026 games, a nearly 30-yard-per-game reduction), while its pass defense has slipped (17th to 23rd).
