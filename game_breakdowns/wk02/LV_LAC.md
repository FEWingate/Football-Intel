## 5. THREAT INTELLIGENCE

**How the Threat system works:** A Threat designation fires only when two conditions converge in the *same* statistical category — a player's own season-long rank in that category clears a threshold, *and* the specific upcoming opponent's defensive rank in that same category is weak enough to clear a matching threshold. Neither strong individual production alone nor a soft matchup alone is sufficient; the system requires both signals to be true simultaneously. The three tiers use fixed thresholds: **Nuclear** requires the player ranked top 3 in the category and the opposing defense ranked 30th-or-worse (among the three weakest in the league) in that same category. **Elite** requires top 5 and a defense ranked 28th-or-worse. **Standard** requires top 10 and a defense ranked 23rd-or-worse. "Double," "Triple," and "Quadruple" labels (not relevant here, since nothing fired) describe how many categories converged for a given player.

**No Threat designation fired for any evaluated starter on either side of this game** — Kirk Cousins, Ashton Jeanty, Tre Tucker, Jack Bech, and Brock Bowers for Las Vegas; Justin Herbert, Omarion Hampton, Ladd McConkey, Tre Harris, and Oronde Gadsden II for Los Angeles.
