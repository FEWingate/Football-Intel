The three tiers use these exact thresholds (player rank is 1st-best; defense rank is 1st-toughest, so a high defense-rank number means a weak defense in that category):

- **Nuclear** — player ranks top 3 AND the opponent's defense ranks 30th or worse in that category
- **Elite** — player ranks top 5 AND opponent defense ranks 28th or worse
- **Standard** — player ranks top 10 AND opponent defense ranks 23rd or worse

"Double," "Triple," and "Quadruple" reflect how many categories converged for that player — Double means one category cleared both thresholds.
